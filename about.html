About Kiwi

Nature-Inspired Abstract Artist

Bringing the beauty of nature to life through dynamic colors, diverse mediums, and unique perspectives, capturing the essence of the natural world in a way that resonates deeply with viewers.

Each piece is carefully crafted to highlight the intricate details of our surroundings, connections, and experiences, using a vibrant palette that infuses energy and emotion into the work.

Whether through the fluidity and texture of oil paints, or the striking contrast of mixed media, Kiwi's Art pushes boundaries and invites the audience to see nature from new, unexpected angles.

Process & Methods

How the work gets made

Starting from the world around us

The work begins outside — not in the studio. A coastline at low tide, light breaking through cloud cover, the way frost settles on a branch overnight. These aren't just visual references; they're the emotional starting point. I sit with a scene long enough that something shifts — a feeling more than a photograph — and that's what gets brought to canvas.

Oil and acrylic, working together

Most pieces combine oil and acrylic rather than treating them as two separate disciplines. Acrylics go down first — fast-drying, allowing bold base layers and broad gestures that establish the composition's energy. Oil comes in slower, building depth and allowing richer blending where the painting asks for it. The tension between the two mediums is part of what gives the work its texture: some areas smooth and luminous, others raw and immediate.

Every mark is intentional

There's no accident here. Each stroke — its direction, weight, where it starts and stops — is a deliberate decision. Painting for me is closer to a physical conversation with the canvas than a technical exercise. The movement of a brushstroke can carry the feeling of a wave breaking, or the quiet stillness of a frozen field, in a way that no amount of literal representation can. I want the viewer to feel the motion, not just see the image.
