Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026 · Applies to kiwisart.com/tally

Tally Tiles is a free daily domino puzzle by Troy Evans. You can play without an account, and we collect as little as possible.

Stored only in your browser

  • Your puzzle progress, times, hints used and best times.
  • The name you last typed for the leaderboard, so you don't have to type it again.

This stays in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Leaderboard (only if you post a score)

When you choose to post a score, we store the name you type, your time, the number of hints you used, the puzzle date and level, and a random ID created by the game in your browser. The random ID only stops the same browser from posting the same puzzle twice; it isn't linked to your identity.

Names and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please don't use your full name or other personal information as your leaderboard name. Leaderboard data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see how the game is used, for example which levels are started and finished. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology, and we ask Google to anonymize IP addresses. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker. See Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

The game may show an ad from Google AdSense between levels. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Learn how in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and turn off personalized ads in My Ad Center. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for consent first.

What we don't do

  • No accounts and no email collection.
  • We don't sell your personal data.

Questions or removing a score

To ask about this policy, or to have a leaderboard score you posted removed, reach us through the kiwisart.com contact page. For a score, include the name, date and level.
