Tally Tiles Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026. Applies to the Tally Tiles website (kiwisart.com/tally).

Tally Tiles is a daily puzzle game by Troy Evans. You can play without an account, and we collect as little as possible.

Stored on your device only

  • Your puzzle progress, times, hints used and best times.
  • The name you last typed for the leaderboard, so you don't have to type it again.
  • In the app, your daily reminder setting.

This stays in your browser or on your device. Clearing your browser data or deleting the app removes it.

Leaderboard (only if you post a score)

When you choose to post a score, we store: the name you type, your time, the number of hints you used, the puzzle date and level, and a random ID created by the game on your device. The random ID only stops the same device from posting the same puzzle twice; it isn't linked to your identity. Names and scores are shown publicly on the leaderboard, so please don't use your full name or personal information as your leaderboard name.

Leaderboard data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

Website analytics

The website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see how the game is used (for example, which levels are started and finished). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology. We ask Google to anonymize IP addresses. You can block it with your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker. See Google's privacy policy.

The app does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics or tracking.

Notifications (app only)

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no email collection and no ads.
  • We don't sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Removing a leaderboard score

To have a score you posted removed, contact us with the name, date and level, and we'll delete it.
