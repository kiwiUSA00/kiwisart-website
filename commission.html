The Process

How it works

From first conversation to finished piece, here's what to expect.

01

Tell me your idea

Send a message describing the scene, memory, or feeling you have in mind. Include any reference images or color notes — the more you share, the better.

02

Consultation

We'll discuss size, medium, palette, and timeline. You'll receive a quote and a simple agreement before any work begins.

03

Creation

Kiwi paints your piece. You'll receive progress photos at key stages and have a chance to weigh in before the final details are locked in.

04

Delivery

Once you're happy with the result, the painting is finished, signed, and shipped — carefully packaged to arrive in perfect condition.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a commission take?

Most commissions take 4–8 weeks from the consultation to completed painting. Timeline varies depending on size and complexity — this will be confirmed before work begins.

What subjects do you work with?

Nature-inspired scenes are a specialty — coastlines, water, weather, seasons, and the quiet energy of the natural world. Abstract pieces inspired by a mood or memory are also welcome.

Do you work in oil or mixed media?

Both are available. Oil paint gives rich texture and depth; mixed media allows for more experimental surfaces. Happy to recommend one based on your vision.

What is the pricing?

Pricing depends on size, medium, and complexity. Get in touch with your idea and you'll receive a quote with no obligation.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes — a 50% deposit is required to begin work, with the remainder due on completion before shipping.

Ready to get started?

Describe your idea and Kiwi will get back to you within a few days to talk it through.
